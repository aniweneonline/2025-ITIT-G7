Mental Health Information

What Is Mental Health?

Mental health refers to a person’s emotional, psychological, and social well-being. It affects how people think, feel, and behave. It also influences how someone handles stress, makes decisions, manages daily responsibilities, and interacts with others. Good mental health does not mean constant happiness, it means being able to function, adapt, and cope with challenges when they come.

Common Signs Someone May Be Struggling

  • Changes in sleep patterns or appetite
  • Difficulty concentrating or making decisions
  • Feeling overwhelmed, empty, or constantly stressed
  • Loss of interest in usual activities
  • Social withdrawal or isolation
  • Unusual mood changes, irritability, or emotional numbness

Why Awareness Matters

Many people ignore mental health until symptoms become severe. Early awareness helps people notice changes faster, understand what they’re experiencing, and seek help before things escalate. Knowing the basics also reduces stigma and allows friends, classmates, and families to respond with support instead of judgment.

When to Seek Help

A person should reach out for support if their thoughts, emotions, or behaviour begin to interfere with daily life, relationships, school, or work. Professional help is not only for “serious cases”, it can simply mean learning healthier coping skills, getting clarity, or talking to someone trained to understand what’s going on.
